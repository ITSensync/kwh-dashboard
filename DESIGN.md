# Dashboard design direction

## Owner direction

Light, tidy, easy-to-scan monitoring for a household energy meter.

## Dials

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Decisions

- The warm neutral page keeps the screen calm; deep green marks the latest meter reading, while amber is reserved for the primary action.
- Geist stays because it is already part of the project and keeps Indonesian labels and changing meter values easy to scan.
- The latest reading leads the page because it is the first decision point; top-up history and its entry form follow as separate tasks.
- Related meter details share a compact grid, while history uses rows suited to comparing dates and amounts.
- The signal card pairs RSSI with a four-level Wi-Fi mark and a text label so connection quality can be scanned without relying on color.
- Motion is limited to short interaction transitions so changing live readings remain the focus.
